Frequently Asked

What happens if my visa is refused?

If you have a past refusal, we perform a strict review of your previous application to find what went wrong. We then fix the gaps in your documentation, update your SOP, and help you reapply with a much stronger file.

How long does visa processing take?

This depends on the destination country's current processing times — we'll give you a realistic estimate during consultation.
